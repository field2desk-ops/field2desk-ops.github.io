FAQ

Common Questions From Shop Owners

Clear answers on how remote dispatching plugs into your day-to-day workflow.

Do we have to change our phone number or field service software?

Not at all. We log directly into the exact tools you already use—Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Quo (OpenPhone), or RingCentral. Customers keep calling your local business number.

Will callers feel like they are speaking to a generic call center?

No. Unlike big answering services where random agents read off a generic script, you get a dedicated coordinator trained specifically on your service zones, pricing tiers, and brand tone.

How do you handle emergency calls vs. routine maintenance?

During onboarding, we map out your exact triage rules—defining what qualifies as a same-day emergency (no-heat, active leaks, extreme-heat AC failure), which tech takes priority, and when to escalate to you.

How fast can we get someone trained on our board?

Once we complete a 15-minute alignment call to review your booking rules and CRM permissions, we typically finish onboarding and go live on your phones within 3 to 5 business days.
