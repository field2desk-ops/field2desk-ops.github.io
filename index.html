Common Questions From Shop Owners

Straight answers on how remote dispatching actually works day-to-day.

Do we have to switch our phone number or CRM software?

Not at all. We log directly into the tools you already pay for—whether that's Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Quo (OpenPhone), RingCentral, or Google Workspace. Your customers keep calling your exact local business number.

Will callers feel like they're talking to a scripted call center?

No. You get a dedicated coordinator trained specifically on your company's service areas, pricing structure, and brand tone—not a shared pool of random agents reading off a generic script.

How do you know how to dispatch our specific technicians?

During our quick onboarding call, we map out your service zones, which techs handle installs vs. service calls, drive-time buffers, and emergency escalation rules so your board stays tight.

How fast can we get someone on our phones?

Once we complete a 15-minute workflow audit and align on your booking rules, we can typically have your coordinator trained on your CRM and answering calls within 3 to 5 business days.
