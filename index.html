        Guide · 2 min

        What a missed call really costs your shop

        A simple way to put a dollar figure on voicemail.

        Read the guide

        Pull your phone log for one normal week and count calls that went unanswered or to voicemail. Multiply that count by your average ticket and by the share of live calls you normally book. That is your weekly revenue at risk. Multiply by 4.33 for a month.

        Then add repeat value: a first-time customer who joins your maintenance plan is worth more than one job. Finally, check callbacks: how many voicemails got returned within 15 minutes? Speed is usually where the lost jobs are.

        Try the numbers in the calculator above.

        Checklist · 3 min

        The emergency call triage checklist

        Six questions every no-cool or leak call should answer.

        Read the checklist
        1. Is the address inside your service area?
        2. What is happening, and since when?
        3. Any safety issue? Gas smell, active flooding, burning smell: follow your company safety policy first, and advise the caller to contact emergency services when needed.
        4. Equipment type and approximate age.
        5. Who will be home, and is access clear?
        6. Confirm your diagnostic fee and the arrival window.
        Playbook · 3 min

        A 48-hour estimate follow-up cadence

        Turn quiet quotes into booked installs.

        Read the playbook
        1. Same day: text the homeowner a thank-you with the estimate link.
        2. Day 1: a quick text asking if they have questions.
        3. Day 2: a friendly call about options, financing, and warranty.
        4. Day 4: send a short note with a review or a similar completed job.
        5. Day 7: a final check-in, then log the outcome (yes, no, or later).
        FAQ

        Common questions from shop owners

        Do we have to change our phone number or software?

        No. We log into the tools you already use, such as Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Quo, or RingCentral. Customers keep calling your local number.

        Will callers feel like they reached a call center?

        We learn your service zones, pricing, and tone, and answer in your company's name, so callers reach what feels like your own front desk.

        How do you handle emergencies vs. routine calls?

        During onboarding we map your triage rules: what counts as same-day, which tech gets priority, and when to escalate to you.

        How much does it cost?

        Pricing depends on your fleet size and the hours you want covered, from part-time overflow to a full-shift desk. We scope it on a 30-minute call and give you a clear quote.

        Is there a long contract?

        Terms are agreed in writing during onboarding. We'll walk through them on the intro call so there are no surprises.

        What hours do you cover?

        We schedule coverage around your US business hours and your time zone, and plan peak windows with you.

        What happens if your coordinator is out?

        Backup coverage is part of the scope conversation, so you know exactly how gaps are handled before we start.

        Do you record calls?

        We can review call recordings for quality in the first weeks, using your phone system and following the recording notices and laws that apply to your state.

        How is my customer data protected?

        We sign an NDA before access, work under a limited dispatcher login, and never need banking or payroll access.

        How fast can we go live?

        After a 30-minute alignment call, onboarding typically takes about 4 business days.
