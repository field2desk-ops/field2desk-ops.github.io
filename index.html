FAQ

Straight Answers for Shop Owners

Everything you need to know about plugging a remote coordinator into your daily operation.

Do we need to change our phone number or field service software?

No. We log directly into the exact tools you already use—Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, Quo (OpenPhone), or RingCentral. Customers keep calling your local number and never notice a handoff.

Will callers feel like they're speaking to an outsourced call center?

Not at all. Unlike generic answering services where random agents read off a basic script, you get one dedicated coordinator trained on your service zones, diagnostic pricing, and brand voice.

How do you handle emergency calls vs. routine maintenance?

During onboarding, we set clear triage rules with you—defining what qualifies as a same-day emergency (such as no-heat, active leaks, or AC failure in extreme heat), which tech gets dispatched first, and when to escalate directly to you.

How quickly can we get started?

After a quick 15-minute alignment call to map out your booking rules and CRM permissions, we typically complete training and go live on your phones within 3 to 5 business days.
